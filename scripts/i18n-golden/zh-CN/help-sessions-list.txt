列出已存的会话，最新的在前

用法：qc sessions list [OPTIONS]

选项：
      --data-dir <DATA_DIR>
          数据目录。默认取 XDG 数据目录

      --titles
          Also print the title the sidebar shows for each session.
          
          Reading a title means scanning the session log (the last rename wins), so this is opt-in: a plain listing stays index-only.

      --config <CONFIG>
          配置文件。默认 ~/.config/qcode/config.toml

      --approval-policy <PATH>
          审批策略文件（[[rules]]）。会替换配置文件里的审批规则

  -h, --help
          打印帮助 (see a summary with '-h')
